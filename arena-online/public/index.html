HOW TO PLAY
  1. Goal: be the first player to get 5 kills. The match then ends for everyone and a results page shows the winner.
  2. Play with friends: one person taps Create New Room and shares the 5-letter code. Everyone else types it under Room Code and taps Join Room. It works from any device, 2 to 16 players.
  3. Fight: every hit drains health. At zero you are eliminated, the shooter gets a kill, and you respawn after about 2 seconds.
  4. Controls: move with WASD / arrow keys (or the left pad on a phone), aim with the mouse (phones aim at the nearest opponent), and hold click or the FIRE button to shoot.
